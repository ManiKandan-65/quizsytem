About QuizSystem

QuizSystem is an interactive learning and assessment platform that helps users test their knowledge across programming and computer science topics. It offers multiple categories, difficulty levels, timed tests, instant feedback, hints, step-by-step explanations, performance analytics, and weak-area practice sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about QuizSystem.

How does the quiz work? ➕
Select a category and difficulty level, answer questions using the interactive palette, and receive instant score metrics and step-by-step explanations upon submission.
Can I choose a category and difficulty? ➕
Yes! You can choose from Java, OOP, DBMS, SQL, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, OS, and Computer Networks across Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulties.
Is there a timer for the quiz? ➕
Yes, you can choose 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 15 minutes, or No Timer mode.
Are my results and history saved? ➕
Yes, your attempt history and leaderboard rankings are saved locally in your browser via LocalStorage.
