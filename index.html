About QuizSystem

QuizSystem is an interactive learning and assessment platform that helps users test their knowledge across programming and computer science topics. It offers customizable multiple categories, difficulty levels, timed quizzes, instant results, and local performance tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about QuizSystem.

How does the quiz work? ➕
Select a category and difficulty level, answer multiple-choice questions within the timer duration, and view instant score metrics upon submission.
Can I choose a category and difficulty? ➕
Yes! You can choose from Java, HTML & CSS, JavaScript, DBMS, OOP, and Computer Science across Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.
Is there a timer for the quiz? ➕
Yes, you can select between 5-minute, 10-minute, or No Timer options during quiz setup.
Are my results and history saved? ➕
Your results and leaderboard rankings are automatically saved locally in your browser using LocalStorage.
Can I retry a quiz? ➕
Absolutely! Questions and options are randomized on every new attempt so you can practice as many times as you like.
Does the website require an account or registration? ➕
No account or signup is required. You can simply enter your name handle and start playing immediately.
